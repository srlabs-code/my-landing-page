Lantern Vault Publisher

The private publishing tool of The Lantern Vault, a YouTube channel of folklore, horror and unsolved mysteries.

What it is

Lantern Vault Publisher is a small script that runs on the channel owner's own computer. When a video is finished, it uploads it to The Lantern Vault's YouTube channel with its title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions and scheduled publish time, adds it to a playlist on the same channel, and can post the channel's own pinned question as a comment once the video is public.

Who uses it

Only the owner of The Lantern Vault, signed in with their own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else, has no other users, and does not read, collect or display anyone else's data.
