Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

In short: ScanBee processes your documents, scans, photos and text on your device. We do not upload them, we cannot see them, and we do not run servers that receive them. No account is needed. The app does use third-party services for ads, analytics, crash reports and in-app purchases; this policy explains exactly what those services receive.

This Privacy Policy explains how SR Labs ("we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use ScanBee: PDF Scanner & Editor (the "App", package name com.srlabs.scanbee) on Android. If you have questions, contact us at srlabs.app@gmail.com.

1. Your Documents Stay on Your Device

Every document feature in the App runs on your device:

  • Scanning and image processing: edge detection, cropping, filters, book-page flattening and ID-card layouts use on-device computer-vision and AI models that ship inside the App.
  • Text recognition (OCR) and barcode / QR scanning: performed on your device with Google ML Kit's bundled on-device models. Your images are not sent to a server for recognition.
  • PDF tools: creating, viewing, signing, annotating, merging, splitting, organizing, rotating, cropping, compressing, converting, protecting, watermarking and numbering are all performed on your device.
  • Translate: translation runs on your device with Google ML Kit. The first time you use a language, its language pack is downloaded from Google once; after that, translation works offline. Your text is not sent for translation.
  • AI Summary: on supported phones, summaries are produced by Gemini Nano running on your device through Android's AICore system service. On other phones, the App uses its own offline key-points method. Your document text is not uploaded in either case.

We never upload, store, view or have access to your scans, photos, PDFs, extracted text, signatures, translations, summaries or scanned codes.

2. Information Stored Only on Your Device

To make the App work, it keeps some information in its private storage on your device. This information is not sent to us:

  • Your recent files list (file names and locations), folders, stars and tags you add, and a search index of text inside your documents so you can search them.
  • Saved signatures, barcode / QR history, and an unfinished scan so you can resume it.
  • Your settings (theme, language, save location, App Lock, reminders) and your tool usage, which orders the tools on the Home screen.
  • A cached copy of your purchase status so Pro features work offline.

Output files are saved to the folder you choose, or to your device's Documents / Downloads folder by default. The App does not delete or change your original files; every tool creates a new file. Uninstalling the App deletes its private data.

Device backup: if Android backup is turned on, Android may back up the App's settings to your own Google account. Your recent files list, purchase cache, scan session and document copies are excluded from backup.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases of ScanBee Pro are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card or billing details. The App receives only what it needs to unlock Pro: the product you bought and its status. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play at any time.

6. How We Use Information

We use the limited information described above only to:

  • provide and improve the App and fix crashes;
  • show ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch, and measure them;
  • unlock Pro features you have bought or earned;
  • comply with law and prevent fraud and abuse.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your documents for any purpose, including advertising or training AI models.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored on your device stays until you delete it in the App (for example, by clearing recent files or deleting a saved signature), clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App. Information held by the third-party services above is kept according to their own retention policies; for example, Firebase Analytics data is kept for a limited period set in our project settings. Because we have no accounts and no servers that hold your content, there is no ScanBee account to delete. For questions about data held by third-party services, contact us and we will help.

8. Security

Your documents remain in your device's storage and in folders you choose. App Lock can protect the App with your device's biometric or screen lock, and a secure-screen option can hide the App from screenshots and the recent-apps preview. Third-party services transmit data using encryption in transit (HTTPS). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep the information we handle to a minimum.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU and UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or US state privacy laws such as the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising. Because your documents never leave your device, most of your information is already under your control. To use your rights for information held by the services above, change your ad and consent settings as described in section 3, or contact us.
