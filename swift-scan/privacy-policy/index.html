Swift PDF (package com.srlabs.swiftpdfpro) is a document scanner and PDF toolkit published by SR Labs (“we”, “us”, “our”) on Google Play. This policy explains what information the app handles, what stays on your device, and what is shared with third-party services.

1Your documents stay on your device

All core features of Swift PDF run entirely on your device:

  • Scanning and image processing (edge detection, cropping, filters, page flattening) is performed locally using on-device computer-vision and machine-learning models.
  • Text recognition (OCR) and barcode/QR scanning are performed locally using Google ML Kit's on-device APIs. Your images are not sent to a server for recognition.
  • PDF creation, merging, splitting, compression, and viewing are performed locally.
We do not upload, store, view, or have any access to your scanned images, photos, PDFs, extracted text, or barcode contents. We do not operate a backend server for document processing.

2Your files — access, storage & deletion

  • The app only reads files you explicitly select through the Android system file picker or share sheet, and images you capture with the camera inside the app.
  • Output files (PDFs, images) are saved to the folder you choose, or to your device's Downloads folder by default.
  • The app never deletes or modifies your original files. Creating a compressed, merged, split, or scanned output always produces a new file; source files are left untouched.
  • A “Recent files” list (file names and locations only) is stored locally on your device so you can quickly reopen documents. You can clear it at any time from the Home screen.
  • Temporary working files created during scanning are stored in the app's private cache and are automatically cleaned up. Uninstalling the app removes all app-private data.

3Permissions the app requests

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, SMS, call logs, or any background data access.

4On-device AI model download

Some scanning enhancements (book-page flattening) use an on-device AI model. If the model was not bundled with your app install, the app downloads it once over the internet (from Google Play's asset delivery or a public model repository). This is a plain file download; no personal data or document content is transmitted, though the download request necessarily exposes your IP address to the hosting server, as with any internet download.

5Third-party services

The app integrates the following Google services. These services may collect certain device information as described in their own privacy policies:

Google AdMob (advertising)

We show optional rewarded ads (for example, to unlock a premium feature once). AdMob may collect and use:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable identifier)
  • IP address and coarse, IP-derived location
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size)
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks)

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads. Where required by law (e.g., EEA/UK), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Google Firebase (Analytics & Remote Config)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand aggregate, anonymous usage (e.g., which tools are used most, crash-free rate, device types) so we can improve the app, and Firebase Remote Config to adjust app settings (such as whether a discount offer is active) without an update. Firebase may collect:

  • A pseudonymous app-instance identifier
  • Device and OS information
  • Coarse usage events (screen views, feature usage counts)

Learn more: Firebase Privacy and Security.

Analytics never includes the content of your documents, file names, extracted text, or images.

Google Play services / ML Kit

On-device text recognition and barcode scanning are provided by ML Kit. Recognition runs locally; ML Kit may contact Google servers only to download or update its on-device models and to report diagnostic information about the SDK itself. Learn more: ML Kit terms.

We do not use any other third-party SDKs, data brokers, or tracking networks.

6Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect, store, or share:

  • Your documents, scans, photos, or PDFs
  • OCR-extracted text or barcode/QR contents
  • File names or file contents
  • Your name, email address, phone number, or contacts
  • Your precise location
  • Any special-category (sensitive) personal data

Because we do not collect your documents, we cannot recover them for you — please keep your own backups of important files.

7Data retention & your choices

  • On-device data (settings, recent-files list, cached scans): remains on your device until you clear it in the app, clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Analytics data (Firebase): retained by Google per Firebase's standard retention controls (up to 14 months for user-level data), in aggregate/pseudonymous form.
  • Advertising data (AdMob): governed by Google's policies; you may reset your Advertising ID at any time.
  • You may contact us at the email below with any privacy request (access, deletion of analytics identifiers, questions). For data processed by Google (AdMob/Firebase), we will direct the request to the appropriate Google mechanism.

8Security

Your documents are processed in the app's private, sandboxed storage, which other apps cannot access. Files you export are placed where you choose and are protected by your device's own security (screen lock, encryption). Network connections made by the app use HTTPS.

9Children's privacy

Swift PDF is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are served through Google AdMob in accordance with Google Play's Families and ads policies. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will address it.

10International users (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA)

  • Legal bases (EEA/UK): ads and analytics rely on your consent where required; core on-device functionality involves no processing of personal data by us.
  • Your rights: depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to processing of personal data. Because we hold no personal data on our own servers, these rights primarily apply to data held by Google (see the links in Section 5); we will assist with any request sent to our contact email.
  • CCPA (California): we do not sell your personal information. Advertising-related data sharing with Google may qualify as “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising under the CPRA; you can opt out of personalized ads via your device's Ads settings and, where shown, the in-app consent options.
