Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 02, 2026

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community at Vidmine ("Company", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy notice, or our practices with regards to your personal information, please contact us.

This privacy notice describes how we might collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your information when you use our application (the "App").

1. What Information Do We Collect?

Personal Information You Disclose to Us
We do not require you to register an account or provide identifiable personal information (like your name or email) to use the core features of the App.

Information Automatically Collected
Some information is collected automatically when you use our App. This information does not reveal your specific identity but may include device and usage information, such as your IP address, browser and device characteristics, operating system, language preferences, referring URLs, device name, country, location, and information about how and when you use our App.

2. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. We use the following services:

  • Google Play Services: For core Android functionality.
  • AdMob (Google): For serving advertisements. AdMob may use device identifiers and personalized advertising data.
  • Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics: For monitoring app stability and anonymous usage statistics to improve performance.

3. How Do We Process Your Information?

We process your information to provide, improve, and administer our App, communicate with you, for security and fraud prevention, and to comply with law. We process your information only when we have a valid legal reason to do so. Importantly, we do not track, log, or store your browsing history or the URLs of the videos you download on any of our servers. All video processing and downloading occurs locally on your device.

4. App Permissions

To function correctly, the App may request certain permissions on your device:

  • Storage / Media: To save downloaded videos directly to your device and to manage files within the Private Safe (Vault).
  • Network / Internet: To browse websites and download videos.

5. Do We Use Cookies and Other Tracking Technologies?

Yes, the built-in browser within the App uses cookies strictly to allow websites you visit to function correctly (e.g., keeping you logged in to a site). You can clear these cookies at any time via the App's Settings menu.

6. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

We keep your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this privacy notice unless otherwise required by law. Since we do not maintain accounts or store data on our servers, data retention primarily applies to the local data on your device, which you can delete at any time.

7. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

We have implemented appropriate and reasonable technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. Your downloaded files stored in the "Private Safe" are secured locally on your device.

8. Changes to This Privacy Notice

We may update this privacy notice from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Last updated" date and the updated version will be effective as soon as it is accessible.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this notice, you may contact us via the support email listed on our Google Play Store listing.
